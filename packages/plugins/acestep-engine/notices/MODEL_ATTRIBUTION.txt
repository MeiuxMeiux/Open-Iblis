Conversion and attribution boundary
  Serveurperso publishes these files as pre-quantized GGUF models for
  acestep.cpp. The embedding was converted from safetensors and quantized to
  Q8_0; the ACE-Step LM is likewise distributed here as Q8_0 GGUF. The
  immutable artifact revision proves the exact distributed bytes, but the
  artifact repository does not record exact input revisions or a conversion
  attestation. This notice therefore does not claim a cryptographically
  complete source-to-GGUF chain.
